## Finalized Novelty Statement

The finalized novelty of this work is a mango-specific, Malihabad-focused, microbiome-aware, explainable AI framework that integrates soil physicochemical variables, climate context, orchard metadata, microbial diversity, taxonomic abundance, functional microbial groups, and agronomic outcome targets to predict mango yield, disease risk, and nutrient availability in a way that can be interpreted and translated into orchard-level recommendations.

## Phase 1 Positioning Decision

The current CSV is suitable for prototyping the computational pipeline, but it is synthetic. Therefore, Phase 1 treats existing model-performance claims in `Final_Research_Report.md` as unverified until reproducible scripts, split definitions, saved models, evaluation outputs, and real-sample validation are added in later phases.

## Ready for Phase 2

Phase 1 is complete when the project team accepts:

- The literature-backed research gap.
- The final objective and research-question set.
- The technical workflow and validation logic.
- The boundary between synthetic prototype work and future real-field validation.
